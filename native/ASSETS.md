# Shortcut logos

The bundled Netflix and YouTube SVG paths come from
[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons),
under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
The files use local colors and cropped view boxes. The YouTube triangle is white.

The Steam Machine asset reproduces the simple blue-disc and dark-ring product mark
shown on [Valve's Steam Machine page](https://store.steampowered.com/sale/steammachine).
It uses only basic SVG shapes and a local color gradient.

The logos identify their respective services and products. Trademark rights remain
with their owners.

The UI bundles these assets in its Qt resource archive. Logo display does not
depend on an internet connection or the TV's app artwork cache.
